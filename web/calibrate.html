This page needs a secure connection for the camera

Browsers only allow camera access on HTTPS. Open the HTTPS version of this page. Your phone will warn that the connection is not private, because the relay makes its own certificate. That is expected on your own network: tap Advanced (Android) or Show Details → visit this website (iPhone) to continue.

Before you start

  1. On the device that feeds the TV (the one connected to the Sync Box), open and make it full screen. It turns the TV black and white by itself.
  2. Dim the room lights. The LEDs and TV must be much brighter than the room.
  3. Stand in front of the TV, 2–4 m away, holding the phone upright and steady (a stand or shelf is best). Keep the whole TV and the LEDs in view.
  4. The TV will show nine small squares, then go white. They are used to measure the TV exactly, so nothing else bright should be in view.

Automatic mode lights the whole strip once to find which LEDs the camera can see. Give a range if some of your LEDs are out of view. It then measures the part next to the TV more finely, so the whole thing takes about 2–3 minutes.

Aim the camera, then press Start.
